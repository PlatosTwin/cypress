            <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-field-text-info field--type-text-long field--label-hidden field__item"><p>In 2007, The <a href="https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_publicworks/0-0-0-4066">Urban Forestry Ordinance of the Public Works Code</a> was revised by the Board of Supervisors to protect certain trees on private property that are close to the public right-of-way.</p><p>Significant Trees are located on private property, where any portion of the tree’s trunk is within 10 feet of the public right-of-way and meet any one of the following size requirements:</p><ul style="margin-left:40px;"><li><div>20 feet or greater in height,</div></li><li><div>15 feet or greater canopy width, or</div></li><li><div>12 inches or greater diameter of trunk measured at 4.5 feet above grade.</div></li></ul><p>&nbsp;</p><p>These trees are granted the same protections as street trees, and a <a href="/services/remove-street-tree">permit is required before any significant tree can be removed</a>. Permits are required for planting or removing street trees and significant trees.</p><p>Landmark trees are trees that have been designated by the Board of Supervisors as extra special.&nbsp; It may be due to the rareness of the species, their size or age, or extraordinary structure, or ecological contribution.&nbsp; In addition, historical or cultural importance can qualify a tree for Landmark Status.&nbsp; Property owners, any member of the Board of Supervisors, Planning Commission, the Landmarks Advisory Board, and/or directors of a city department may nominate trees on public or private land to protect and preserve their value and presence in the community under the San Francisco Landmark Tree Program.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>Trees that are designated by the city for landmark status are protected from physical damage and removal.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p><strong>List of Designated Landmark Trees</strong></p><ol><li>1801 Bush Street (Adjacent):&nbsp;Six Blue Gum&nbsp;Eucalyptus (<em>Melaleuca linariifolia</em>)</li><li>555 Battery Street:&nbsp;Sweet Bay (<em>Laurus nobilis</em>)</li><li>1221 Stanyan Street:&nbsp;New Zealand Christmas Tree (M<em>etrosiderous giganteum</em>) </li><li>46A Cook Street: Norfolk-Island Cook Pine Hybrid (<em>Araucaria spp.</em>)</li><li>2251 Filbert St. (Backyard): Canary Island Pine (<em>Pinus canariensis</em>) </li><li><span Terrace (backyard): Coast Redwood (<em>Sequoia sempervirens</em>)</li></ol><p>&nbsp;</p><p>To nominate a remarkable tree, visit the&nbsp;<a
