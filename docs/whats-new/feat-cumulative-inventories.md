# The cumulative-inventories round. Two lines, because two different things changed for a
# tester and neither one says the other.
#
# The first is the map: downloaded cities used to be a choice between one inventory and another,
# and are now all drawn at once. That is the whole of the round and it is what a tester will
# notice first — they can stand in Brooklyn and the trees are there without having gone to a
# screen to say so.
#
# The second is the Cities screen, which is the half the owner actually reported: San Francisco
# and San Jose ship inside the app and were being offered with a `Use` button beside a built-in
# card that simultaneously said it included them. That contradiction is gone along with the verb
# that expressed it.
#
# **`Use` and `In use` are not mentioned**, deliberately. A tester who never noticed them does
# not need to be told a word has left the screen; a tester who did will see that the question it
# asked no longer exists. Naming a removed control in a changelog invites the reading that
# something was taken away.
#
# Both lines are true of the build this merges into: the union is what the app boots on, and the
# screen is what it draws. Neither is behind a flag.

Every city you download is now on the map at the same time — no more choosing which one the map draws.

Cities that ship inside the app are listed under the built-in inventory, and offer an update when a newer record is published rather than a second copy to download.
