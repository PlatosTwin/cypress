# Comment lines and blank lines are ignored by `Tools/whats_new.py compile`.

In the Journal, the Neighborhood and City pages now say where their area came from and let you pick a different one — any neighborhood or city the inventories on your phone cover. And when your phone's location is too rough to tell which neighborhood you're in, the page says so instead of naming one anyway.
