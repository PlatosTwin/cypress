Cypress
Product concept · mobile + web
Visual identity

A field guide in your pocket, named after a windswept tree.

The mark is a Monterey Cypress on its granite headland at sunset: sinuous trunk, layered windswept crown, one bare reaching branch. The palette comes straight from the tree, with canopy greens, bark, fog, and one amber reserved for "this tree needs something."

Mobile · Phase 1

Six screens that carry the whole loop

The loop: explore, identify, visit, care, check in, and report honestly. Every contribution field is optional and everything queues offline. The amber path never pretends the city was notified.

Mobile · one level deeper

The flows behind the first screen

Where taps lead: the species field guide, the collection that makes learning a game, the thirty-second care log, the share sheet that brings the next person in, and the growth history behind every number.

Mobile · in the field

Built for day one and muddy hands

The unglamorous screens that make the app trustworthy: what a brand-new tree looks like before anyone has photographed it, when the account question finally gets asked, how a number enters the record, where unsent work waits out a dead zone, and how a volunteer's morning rolls from tree to tree.

Web · public & shareable

Every tree gets a page worth sending to a friend

No login is needed to view a tree. The share card (best photo, name, season strip) is the growth loop: enthusiasts showing off their trees.
